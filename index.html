About Mohmand Pharmacy

Committed to excellence in pharmaceutical care since 2010

Mohmand Pharmacy was founded in 2010 with a vision to provide accessible, quality healthcare services to our community. We believe in combining traditional pharmacy care with modern technology to serve you better.

Our team of licensed pharmacists and healthcare professionals are dedicated to ensuring your health and well-being. We source our medicines from trusted manufacturers and maintain strict quality standards.
